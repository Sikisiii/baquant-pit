# Origin and authority

BAquant is the private origin project. baquant-pit is intended to become an
independent reusable infrastructure library informed by its temporal research
lessons. This private staging repository contains newly authored scaffolding and
planning only; no authoritative BAquant PIT implementation or history has been
extracted. Source commit/ref and inspection scope are retained in private task
evidence, outside this package's distributable content.

BAquant historical implementation is not automatically the public API. Public
API semantics must be independently reviewed. Historical BAquant Stage documents
do not automatically become baquant-pit authority. New baquant-pit contracts will
require explicit versioning and tests, provenance review and Owner approval for
their scope. Stable origin primitives are candidates, not implied compatibility.

No BAquant business Stage status is inherited by baquant-pit. Tests establish only
their declared technical scope; they do not authorize business acceptance,
extraction, public release, redistribution or market expansion. Origin project
state, permissions and consumer qualifications remain in that private project.

The current authority here is the bounded private bootstrap authorization. The
planning documents describe future work; they do not authorize its execution.
Evolving identity/classification, applicability and consumer-specific vintage
semantics need a fresh independent decision before becoming contracts.

No public redistribution or open-source license is granted. BAquant remains
private. Any extraction, private-to-public change, release or package publication
requires separately scoped explicit Owner authorization and applicable audits.
